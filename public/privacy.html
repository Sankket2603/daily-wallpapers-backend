Privacy Policy for Daily Wallpapers

Effective Date: September 17, 2026 | App: Daily Wallpapers | Contact: sanketsb111@gmail.com
Summary: Daily Wallpapers does NOT require an account, does NOT ask for personal information, and does NOT sell your data. We use network permissions solely to deliver wallpapers and Google AdMob to show optional rewarded video ads when unlocking 4K wallpapers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Daily Wallpapers does not require registration, does not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, precise GPS locations, or access private contacts/photos.

2. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to serve rewarded video ads for unlocking wallpapers. Google AdMob may process device advertising identifiers (Google AAID / Apple IDFA), device model, IP address, and interaction metrics under Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Android Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To stream wallpapers and rewarded video ads.
  • SET_WALLPAPER: To apply wallpapers directly to your Home and Lock screens.
  • Scoped Storage: To save wallpaper images exclusively to the public Pictures/Wallpapers directory.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The application is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You may reset or delete your Advertising ID anytime in your device settings under Settings > Google > Ads.
